SALVAGE — PRESS KIT BIOGRAPHIES: CAST
Harbor Line Pictures

TOBI ADEYEMI — Wale

Long form

Tobi Adeyemi plays Wale, the crew supervisor. Wale decides how long anybody stays down, and Wale is the one holding the contract.

Adeyemi trained for nine weeks with commercial diving instructors before principal photography and performs his own underwater work in every scene he appears in. He had never dived before he was cast.

He came to acting late, at twenty-eight, after six years fitting shopfronts. He's worked mainly in theatre since, and SALVAGE is his second feature.

"The instructors kept telling us the same thing, which is that panic is a decision you make about forty seconds before you make it. You can catch it. That's the entire job, and it's most of what Wale does for a living."

Short form, 45 words

MARTA SILVESTRI — Renata

Long form

Marta Silvestri plays Renata, a diver eight months out of a decompression injury who's back in the water because the money's good.

Silvestri learned to work a full face mask in three days. Her instructor said she was the fastest he'd taught in twenty years of the job, which she's been reminding the rest of the cast about ever since.

She's made eleven features across four countries and speaks Italian, English and Portuguese. She read for Renata twice, and the second time she asked to do it in the tank.

"I didn't want to find out on the day that I couldn't do it. Better to know in September than in March, with a crew waiting."

Short form, 45 words

Marta Silvestri plays Renata, a diver returning to work eight months after a decompression injury. Silvestri has made eleven features across four countries. She learned to work a full face mask in three days and asked to audition in the water.

CILLIAN ROURKE — Sam

Long form

Cillian Rourke plays Sam, who's twenty-three, hasn't ever worked a wreck, and hasn't been told what the job actually pays.

SALVAGE is Rourke's first feature. He was cast from a self-tape recorded in his sister's kitchen, and he found out in the same week that he'd have to learn to dive.

"Nine weeks sounds like a long time until somebody explains what happens if you come up too fast. Then it sounds like nothing at all."

Short form, 40 words

Cillian Rourke plays Sam, who's twenty-three and on his first wreck. SALVAGE is Rourke's first feature. He was cast from a self-tape recorded in his sister's kitchen and learned to dive in nine weeks.
