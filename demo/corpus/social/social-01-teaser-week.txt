FACEBOOK — general

SALVAGE is Ines Kowalczyk's first narrative feature. She spent two years on North Sea vessels making a documentary first, and she wrote this during that edit.

Three commercial divers take a job cutting apart a wreck in the Irish Sea. The contract pays on tonnage. What's in the hold is worth more than the steel.

108 minutes. Grand Jury Prize for Direction and Best Sound at Cascadia.
Limited 30 January. Everywhere 6 February.
