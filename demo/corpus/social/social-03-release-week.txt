LINKEDIN — national release

SALVAGE opens nationally today.

Three commercial divers take a contract cutting apart a wreck in the Irish Sea. The job pays on tonnage, they have sixteen days of workable weather, and the hold isn't empty.

Ines Kowalczyk's first narrative feature. Grand Jury Prize for Direction and Best Sound at Cascadia. Built in a four-hundred-thousand-litre tank at a former shipyard in Ardrossan, with a cast who trained for nine weeks and did their own underwater work.

108 minutes. In cinemas now.
