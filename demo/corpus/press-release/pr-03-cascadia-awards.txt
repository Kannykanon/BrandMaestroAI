SALVAGE Wins Grand Jury Prize for Direction at Cascadia

Wren Okpara's sound design also takes a prize; the film's second screening was held twice in one night

VANCOUVER — The Sunday night screening of SALVAGE started forty minutes late because the queue for standby reached the end of the block.

SALVAGE has won the Grand Jury Prize for Direction at the Cascadia International Film Festival. Wren Okpara won the festival's prize for Best Sound. Both awards were announced at Saturday's ceremony.

The jury citation ran to one sentence: "For a film that trusts its audience to hear what it will not show them."

The festival added a second Sunday screening after the first sold out in nine minutes. Both were full. It's the first time Cascadia has doubled a Competition title mid-festival.

SALVAGE is the first narrative feature from Ines Kowalczyk, whose 2022 documentary Ninety Fathoms was shot over two years on North Sea vessels. The film follows three commercial divers cutting apart a sunken cargo ship in the Irish Sea on a contract that pays by weight.

"I've never made anything for a room before," said Kowalczyk. "Documentaries get watched on laptops mostly. Sitting in the dark on Sunday and hearing four hundred people stop breathing at the same moment — I don't have anything to compare that to."

Okpara spent seven months on the mix, which is longer than he's spent on anything else. Most of the film's underwater sound was recorded live, inside the production's tank at Ardrossan, using hydrophones fixed to the divers' helmets and to the hull of the dressed wreck.

"People assume underwater sound is muffled," said Okpara. "It isn't. It's loud, and it's close, and it's mostly your own body. That was the note for seven months."

Tobi Adeyemi, Marta Silvestri and Cillian Rourke star. The three leads trained for nine weeks with commercial diving instructors and perform their own underwater work.

Harbor Line Pictures holds worldwide rights. The film opens in a twelve-city limited run on 30 January and expands nationally on 6 February.

SALVAGE is produced by Freight & Tide. Nadia Berhe and Owen Pryce produce. Sung-min Ha is the director of photography. Lior Cattan composed the score. Bess Traoré edited. The film runs 108 minutes.

About Harbor Line Pictures

Harbor Line Pictures acquires and distributes narrative features in North America, the United Kingdom and Ireland. The company releases between six and nine films a year.
