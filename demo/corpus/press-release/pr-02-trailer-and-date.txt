FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

SALVAGE Sets 6 February Release, First Trailer Online Today

Harbor Line moves the film up two weeks after Cascadia; limited run opens 30 January

LONDON — The first trailer for SALVAGE is ninety-four seconds long and contains no dialogue for the first sixty of them.

Harbor Line Pictures has confirmed 6 February as the national release date for SALVAGE, Ines Kowalczyk's first narrative feature. A limited run opens in twelve cities on 30 January. The date moves the film two weeks earlier than planned, following its premiere in Competition at the Cascadia International Film Festival, where it won the Grand Jury Prize for Direction and the prize for Best Sound.

The trailer went online this morning.

Kowalczyk cut it herself with editor Bess Traoré over four days in November. She hadn't cut a trailer before. It uses eleven shots. Nine of them were photographed inside the production's tank at Ardrossan, and none of them are from the last twenty minutes of the film.

"Trailers usually tell you what the movie is about," said Kowalczyk. "I wanted this one to tell you what it feels like to be down there and hear somebody breathing next to you. If you know what happens, we've made a mistake."

Wren Okpara's sound design carries most of the cut. Okpara recorded the divers' regulators and umbilical hoses on set rather than replacing them in post, and he built the trailer's mix out of that material.

"There's no score in it," said Okpara. "There's a heartbeat, a compressor, and a woman counting. That's the whole thing."

SALVAGE follows three commercial divers cutting apart a sunken cargo ship in the Irish Sea on a contract that pays by weight. Tobi Adeyemi, Marta Silvestri and Cillian Rourke star. The film runs 108 minutes.

Harbor Line has confirmed the twelve-city limited run for 30 January, with the national expansion following on 6 February. Additional markets will be announced in January.

"We didn't want a long gap between the reviews and the tickets," said Priya Vellani, head of acquisitions at Harbor Line. "People came out of Cascadia wanting to talk about it. We'd rather they could see it while they still want to."

SALVAGE is produced by Freight & Tide. Nadia Berhe and Owen Pryce produce. Sung-min Ha is the director of photography. Lior Cattan composed the score. Bess Traoré edited.

About Harbor Line Pictures

Harbor Line Pictures acquires and distributes narrative features in North America, the United Kingdom and Ireland. The company releases between six and nine films a year.
