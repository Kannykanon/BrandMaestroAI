Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Brand Brain work?
We don"t just use generic RAG. We use a proprietary extraction pipeline that builds a Markdown-based structural blueprint and vectorizes your exact stylistic rhythm to vectorize your uploaded PDFs and text. When generating new content, our retrieval system fetches the most stylistically relevant paragraphs to use as few-shot exemplars, forcing the LLM to adopt your exact tone.
Is my brand data used to train public models?
Absolutely not. Your uploaded documents are isolated in a dedicated Postgres pgvector database assigned solely to your Business ID. We do not use customer data to train foundational models.
What happens when I reject a generation?
When you reject content in the human feedback loop, the system extracts the specific stylistic failure and commits it to a negative constraint database. Future generations are explicitly instructed to avoid these identified anti-patterns.

The Generation Pipeline

A state-of-the-art StateGraph architecture ensuring compliance and brilliance.

1

Ingest & Vectorize

Upload your reference documents. Our asynchronous workers chunk and embed the text into a pgvector-backed Postgres database, completely isolating your brand data from other tenants.

2

Dynamic Blueprint Mapping

When you request new content, the pipeline retrieves the most semantically relevant examples from your past work to use as strict few-shot exemplars, capturing your exact pacing and rhythm.

3

Auditor Verification

An autonomous Auditor node reviews the generated content against your strict brand constraints. If it fails, the Writer is instructed to rewrite it until it hits an 8.0/10 compliance score.
