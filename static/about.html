About BrandMaestro AI

BrandMaestro AI writes content in a brand's own voice, and can turn approved scripts into story videos for the brand's YouTube channel.

BrandMaestro AI is operated by BAND AI.

Content writing

A business uploads examples of its approved writing and documents about its products. BrandMaestro AI builds a profile of that voice, then researches, writes and checks blog posts, ads, proposals, scripts and press releases against it. A person reviews every piece and approves or rejects it, and the system learns from that feedback.

YouTube Studio (optional)

Businesses that want video can turn an approved script into a narrated story:

  • They create characters, give each a voice, and upload face photos they have the right to use.
  • BrandMaestro AI draws a storyboard, voices the lines, animates speaking characters, adds captions, and renders the video.
  • A person approves the storyboard and watches the finished video before anything leaves the app.

How BrandMaestro AI uses YouTube

The owner connects their own YouTube channel by signing in with Google. BrandMaestro AI then uses the YouTube Data API only to:

  • upload videos the owner made and approved in the app, as private;
  • set those videos' title, description, tags, category, audience, thumbnail and AI-content disclosure;
  • make a video public, or schedule it, when the owner clicks Publish or Schedule;
  • read the channel's name and ID, and the processing and privacy status of videos it uploaded.

It does not read, change or delete other videos, and does not access comments, analytics, subscribers or other Google data. Every uploaded video is marked as containing AI-generated content. The owner can disconnect the channel at any time, which revokes BrandMaestro AI's access.

Google sign-in is used only to connect a YouTube channel. It is not used to sign in to BrandMaestro AI.
